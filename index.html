Social Media Sheet Format Required

Create a separate Google Sheet with this exact header row:
Month,Brand,Platform,Followers,Monthly Growth %,Views,Engagement Rate %,Paid Distribution %,Organic Distribution %

Example data rows:
January,AAFM India,Instagram,12500,4.2,152000,3.8,35,65
January,AAFM India,Facebook,8500,2.1,89000,2.5,45,55
January,AAFM India,LinkedIn,6200,5.7,45000,4.2,25,75

Sheet Columns Required (for new filters)

Add these exact columns in your Google Sheet header row: Department and Head of Department.

Example placement: Course | Department | Head of Department | Budget | Leads | Month | Platform | ...

Brand Logos Setup

To add brand logos to the Social Media Report page, update the brandLogos object in the JavaScript code.

You can use:

  • URLs to online images
  • Base64 encoded images
  • Relative paths to local images
Example:
brandLogos["AAFM India"] = "https://example.com/aafm-logo.png";
